# FodyCloud trademark policy

The FodyCloud **code** is free software under the AGPL-3.0: you can use, study, change and share
it (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **names** "FodyCloud" and "FodyDev" and the FodyCloud logo
(`internal/web/static/brand/`) are different: they tell people who runs a service and who stands
behind it, so FodyDev keeps control of them.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your service or product **is based on FodyCloud**, or **runs FodyCloud**, as long as
  it's true.
- Use the name to refer to the project, in articles, talks, courses, package names and forks'
  READMEs ("a fork of FodyCloud").
- Run an **unmodified** release of FodyCloud under its own name for your school, lab or
  community, if you make clear that you, not FodyDev, operate it (the operator settings and legal
  pages already name you).

## What needs FodyDev's written permission

- Running a **modified** version, or a commercial service, under the FodyCloud name or logo.
- Using the names or the logo in a way that suggests FodyDev operates, endorses or supports your
  service.
- Using "FodyCloud" or "FodyDev" in a domain name, company name or product name.

## If you fork

Pick your own name and logo: replace the brand files and the product name in the templates and
the docs. Keep the copyright notices and the license, as the AGPL requires.

## Contact

Ask FodyDev through the project's issue tracker; we usually say yes to non-commercial and
educational uses.
