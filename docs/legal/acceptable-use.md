# Acceptable-use policy

**Effective date:** %EFFECTIVE_DATE% · **Version:** %LEGAL_VERSION%

FodyCloud is shared by many students and researchers. This policy says what you may not do with
the platform, so that it stays fast, safe and free for everyone. It is part of the
[Terms of service](terms.md).

## You must not use FodyCloud to

- **Mine cryptocurrency**, or run any other long CPU-heavy background job unrelated to a web
  application.
- **Attack or probe other systems:** scanning, brute-forcing, denial of service, exploiting
  vulnerabilities, or trying to get around FodyCloud's isolation and limits.
- **Send spam** or bulk unsolicited messages.
- **Phish or deceive:** fake login pages, impersonating a person, a company, a bank or a
  university, scams.
- **Spread malware**, or host command-and-control servers.
- **Run open proxies, VPNs or anonymizers** for other people's traffic, or relay traffic to hide
  its origin.
- **Host illegal content**, content that sexually exploits minors, content that infringes others'
  rights (pirated software, films, music), or content that incites violence or hatred.
- **Collect personal data** without the people's knowledge and a lawful basis.
- **Abuse the free plan:** creating several accounts to get around its limits, or reselling the
  service.

## What we do about it

- FodyCloud watches resource use automatically: an app that uses all its CPU for a long time is
  flagged, and on the free plan it is stopped if it happens again.
- Depending on how serious the case is, we may warn you, stop an app, block a repository, or
  suspend your account. In urgent or serious cases we act first and tell you after.
- We report illegal activity to the authorities when the law requires it.

## Report abuse

If an app hosted on `fodycloud.example` breaks this policy, write to **%OPERATOR_EMAIL%** with its
address and what you saw. We read every report.
